# Learning Goals

## What I Hope to Learn

During this internship, I hope to gain practical experience in a real working environment and understand how the knowledge I learn at university is applied in an actual company.

I especially want to learn more about cyber security, internal security processes, security audits, and how security issues are identified and reported. I also want to understand how a real software product works from a security and user perspective.

## Skills I Am Most Excited to Develop

The main skills I want to develop are:

- Cyber security and security auditing
- Identifying and documenting security issues
- Vulnerability and risk assessment
- QA testing and finding software issues
- Professional communication and teamwork
- Using security tools and industry practices
- Understanding how security policies are applied in a real company

I am also interested in learning how to investigate problems instead of only following instructions.

## What I Can Contribute to the Team

As an intern, I am still learning, but I can contribute by being willing to research, test things carefully, and document what I find.

I can help with tasks such as QA testing, checking documentation, identifying usability issues, reviewing security-related information, and reporting findings clearly.

I can also bring a fresh perspective as someone who is experiencing the product from a learner and first-time-user point of view.

## An Area I Feel Less Confident In

One area I am less confident in is my practical cyber security experience. I have learned security concepts through my studies, but I am still developing my ability to apply them in real-world situations.

I would like to improve this by working on actual security tasks, learning from feedback, and understanding how experienced people approach security problems.

I also want to become more confident when communicating my findings and asking questions.

## What Success Looks Like for Me

For me, success in this internship would mean finishing the internship with practical skills that I can continue using after it ends.

I want to be able to look at a security or technical problem, understand what is happening, research it properly, and explain my findings clearly.

I would also consider the internship successful if I become more independent, communicate better with a professional team, and gain enough confidence to take on technical tasks without always needing step-by-step instructions.

Most importantly, I want to finish the internship knowing that I have actually improved my skills and contributed something useful to the team.
