# Company Reflection

## Why I Think Focus Bear Was Created

After exploring the Focus Bear website, I understand that Focus Bear was created to help people stay focused, build productive routines, and reduce distractions. The company describes itself as a focus coach for busy brains and focuses on helping users build routines, block distractions, and stay focused on important tasks.

I think Focus Bear was created because many people know what they need to do but still find it difficult to actually stay focused and complete it. Having a normal to-do list does not always solve this problem. There can still be distractions from social media, websites, notifications, or other things around us.

I also found it interesting that Focus Bear was built from the founder's own experience with ADHD and autism. The idea seems to come from trying to solve a real problem rather than just creating another general productivity application.

## What Problem Is Focus Bear Solving?

I think Focus Bear is mainly trying to solve problems related to distraction, inconsistent routines, and difficulty maintaining focus.

Some of the main areas I noticed are:

- Blocking distracting websites and applications
- Helping users follow routines step by step
- Providing focus sessions
- Encouraging users to take breaks
- Reducing the number of decisions a user has to make
- Helping users stay focused across different devices

One feature I found interesting is that Focus Bear can block distracting sites and applications across different devices. It also guides users through routines one step at a time instead of showing the entire routine at once.

I think this approach can be useful for people who become overwhelmed when they have too many things to think about at the same time.

## Why I Think This Mission Is Important

I think this mission is important because difficulty with focus is not limited to people with a particular diagnosis. Students and working professionals can also struggle with distractions, procrastination, stress, and maintaining consistent routines.

For example, a university student may have an important assignment to complete but keep checking social media or watching videos instead. Even though the student knows the assignment is important, avoiding distractions can still be difficult.

A tool that reduces those distractions can make it easier for the person to concentrate on the task instead of relying only on willpower.

## How Focus Bear Aligns With My Interests

As a Computer Engineering student, I am interested in how technology can be used to solve real-world problems.

I am also interested in software development, AI, and cybersecurity, so working with a software product gives me an opportunity to understand how technology is developed and used outside the university environment.

I find Focus Bear interesting because it combines software with behavioural habits and productivity. It is not only about building a technical feature. The product also has to consider how people actually use it and what makes it easier or harder for them to stay focused.

## A Challenge I Can Relate To

I do not personally know anyone with a diagnosed ADHD or autism condition, so I cannot speak from personal experience about what it is like to live with either condition.

However, I can relate to some of the general problems that Focus Bear is trying to solve. As a university student, I have experienced situations where I needed to study or complete an assignment but became distracted by other things.

Sometimes the problem is not that I do not know what I need to do. The harder part can be starting the task, staying focused, and avoiding distractions for a long enough period to make meaningful progress.

Because of this, I can understand why features such as blocking distracting websites, guided routines, and focus sessions could be useful.

## How Focus Bear Could Help Someone

Even though I do not personally know someone with ADHD or autism, I can imagine how Focus Bear could help a person who struggles with focus or executive functioning.

For example, someone may have several university tasks to complete but feel overwhelmed by having to decide what to do first. Instead of giving them a long list of tasks, a guided routine could show them one step at a time.

Similarly, if they frequently get distracted by social media or other websites while studying, blocking those distractions could make it easier to stay with the task.

I think the important part is that the tool can reduce some of the decisions and distractions that make completing a task harder.

## My Overall Reflection

After learning more about Focus Bear, I think the company is trying to solve a problem that many different types of people can experience, even though its approach is particularly informed by ADHD and autism.

What I found most interesting is that Focus Bear is not simply trying to tell users to be more productive. It tries to change the environment around the user by blocking distractions, guiding routines, and helping users stay focused.

I also learned that productivity is not always about working harder. Sometimes making the environment simpler and removing unnecessary distractions can make it easier to get things done.

Overall, I think Focus Bear is trying to help people spend less effort fighting distractions and more effort on the things that actually matter to them.
