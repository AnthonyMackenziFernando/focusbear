# User Manual for Anthony

## A Little About Me

I am a RMIT student currently doing my internship at Focus Bear. I am still learning and building my experience, so I may not always know the best approach immediately. I usually learn better when I understand why something works instead of just being given the final answer.

## How I Prefer to Communicate

I prefer clear and direct communication. If something needs to be done in a particular way, it helps me to know the expected outcome and any important requirements.

For normal updates and questions, written messages are useful because I can refer back to them later. For something that is difficult to explain through messages, I am comfortable discussing it in a meeting or call.

If I am quiet, it does not necessarily mean that I am not interested. Sometimes I need a little time to understand the information and organise my thoughts before responding.

## What Helps Me Work Well

I work best when I have a clear task and know what the expected result is. Having a few clear steps or examples helps me get started, especially when I am working on something new.

I also prefer having some time to work independently once I understand the task. If I get stuck, I will try to research the problem first before asking for help.

## How I Like Feedback

I prefer honest and clear feedback. If something I have done is incorrect or could be improved, I would rather know directly so I can fix it.

It also helps if the feedback explains what needs to be changed and why. I do not expect everything I do to be perfect, especially while I am learning, so I see feedback as part of improving.

## When I Am Stuck

My usual approach is to first try to understand the problem myself by researching it and checking available resources. If I still cannot figure it out, I will ask a teammate.

If I remain blocked after making a reasonable attempt, I will contact my supervisor rather than spending too much time going in circles.

If I ask a question, it usually means I have already tried to find the answer and need clarification or guidance.

## Things That Help Me Stay Productive

Having a clear task and a small goal to work towards helps me stay motivated. I prefer making steady progress rather than trying to solve everything at once.

Breaking a large task into smaller parts also makes it easier for me to see progress and avoid getting overwhelmed.

## A Few Things to Know About Me

- I am still building my practical industry experience, so I may need clarification when I encounter something completely new.
- I learn better when I understand the reasoning behind a task.
- I appreciate clear expectations and examples when learning a new process.
- I am comfortable asking questions when I do not understand something.
- I prefer to solve problems independently first before asking for help.
- I appreciate direct feedback because it helps me improve.
- If I make a mistake, I would rather understand what went wrong and learn from it than simply have the mistake corrected for me.

## What You Can Expect From Me

I will try to communicate my progress regularly, take responsibility for my tasks, and ask for help when I am genuinely blocked.

I will also try to be respectful of other people's time and communication styles. If I am unsure about something, I will ask rather than make assumptions.

## In Short

The easiest way to work with me is to give me a clear goal, let me understand the problem, and give me some space to work on it. If I am going in the wrong direction, direct feedback is welcome. I am still learning, so I value people who are willing to explain things and give me the opportunity to improve.
