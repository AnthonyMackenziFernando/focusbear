# Internship Time Plan

## 1. Goal

My goal is to manage my internship time properly, complete the assigned tasks, and gain practical experience in software testing, cybersecurity, auditing, and product research.

I will follow a weekly schedule and review my progress at the end of each week. I will also use Focus Bear to record my focus sessions while working on internship tasks.

## 3. Week-by-Week Plan

I will repeat the weekly schedule throughout my internship, while changing the activities based on the tasks assigned to me.

### Week 1: Planning and Task Review
- Review internship requirements and expected working hours.
- Check assigned tasks and their deadlines.
- Set up Focus Bear and begin recording genuine work sessions.
- Organise a task list and identify priorities.

### Week 2: Product Familiarisation and Testing
- Explore Focus Bear from a new user's perspective.
- Test the onboarding process and main features.
- Record genuine bugs, confusing steps, and possible usability improvements.
- Submit findings through the appropriate reporting process.

### Week 3: Cybersecurity and Policy Learning
- Review assigned cybersecurity guidelines and company policies.
- Understand the purpose of relevant security controls.
- Record findings and practical recommendations.
- Ask for clarification when a requirement is unclear.

### Week 4: Audit and Documentation
- Continue assigned security audit or research tasks.
- Organise evidence and document observations.
- Review whether findings are supported by the available evidence.
- Update reports and task submissions based on feedback.

### Week 5: Feedback and Improvements
- Review feedback from supervisors and the Focus Bear Intern Review Bot.
- Correct incomplete submissions.
- Add missing evidence or explanations where required.
- Check that completed tasks meet their acceptance criteria.

### Week 6 and Beyond: Ongoing Work and Review
- Continue product testing, research, auditing, and documentation based on assigned priorities.
- Attend scheduled meetings and complete follow-up actions.
- Review progress against the required internship hours.
- Identify areas where I need further learning or guidance.
- Adjust the weekly plan when new tasks or deadlines arise.

## 4. Focus Bear Time Tracking

I will use Focus Bear to record my internship work sessions.

My process will be:

1. Start a focus session when I begin a genuine internship task.
2. Work on the selected task during the session.
3. Pause or stop the session when I take a break or finish working.
4. Record my progress and any relevant outcomes.
5. Review my recorded sessions at the end of each week.

I will use the recorded sessions to help track my working time. I understand that focus-session records should reflect my actual work, and I will not count breaks or unrelated activities as internship hours.

## 5. Weekly Progress Review

At the end of each week, I will check:

- [ ] Did I follow my planned schedule?
- [ ] Did I record my actual work sessions in Focus Bear?
- [ ] Did I make progress on my assigned tasks?
- [ ] Did I submit completed work and respond to feedback?
- [ ] Are any tasks blocked or waiting for clarification?
- [ ] Do I need to adjust next week's schedule?

I will also keep a short weekly update describing the tasks I worked on, the progress I made, and what I plan to do next.

## 6. Managing Missed Sessions

If I cannot work during a planned time slot, I will try to move the work to another available time during the same week.

If I fall behind, I will review the remaining tasks, prioritise urgent deadlines, and arrange additional working sessions where possible. I will update my plan rather than recording hours that I have not actually worked.

## 7. Commitment

I will treat the internship as a regular professional commitment. My aim is to maintain a consistent schedule, track my time honestly, complete tasks to an appropriate standard, and improve my practical skills throughout the internship.

I will confirm the required total internship hours with my supervisor and adjust this plan if the expected weekly commitment is different from the schedule above.
