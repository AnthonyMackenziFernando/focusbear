# Professionalism Reflection

## What I Learned

I learned that professionalism at Focus Bear is not about being overly formal. The company has a relaxed and friendly environment, but there is still an expectation to be respectful, responsible, and accountable for my work.

One thing that stood out to me was the expectation to become more self-directed during the internship. I should not always wait for someone to tell me what to do. If I have an idea about what I should work on next, I can suggest it to my supervisor and confirm that I am heading in the right direction.

## Communication

For remote work, clear communication is especially important because people cannot always see what others are working on.

I should make my messages clear and respectful, especially when asking for help or following up on something. If I disagree with someone, I should focus on the actual work or idea rather than making it personal. I should also ask for clarification if I do not understand something instead of making assumptions.

I also learned that different communication methods should be used depending on how urgent something is. General matters can be handled through email, while more urgent issues can be communicated through Discord or Teams. Truly urgent matters may require SMS or a call.

## Working with Supervisors

Before meetings, I should read the relevant project information, understand what I have already completed, and prepare any questions or points that I need to discuss.

During meetings, I should pay attention instead of multitasking and take notes about important decisions and action items. After the meeting, I should follow up on anything I am responsible for.

If I am stuck, I should first try to solve the problem myself by researching it or using available resources. If I still cannot solve it, I can ask a teammate. If I remain blocked after making a reasonable attempt, I should contact my supervisor rather than staying stuck.

## Being Proactive

I can be proactive by regularly updating my team about my progress instead of waiting for someone to ask. Even if I have not made much progress, communicating the current situation and any blockers helps the team understand where things stand.

If I need information or an action from someone, I should ask clearly and give enough context. If I do not receive a response, I can send a polite follow-up. If the matter is becoming urgent, I should use a more appropriate communication channel instead of repeatedly waiting for an email response.

## Teamwork and Feedback

Good teamwork means listening to other people, respecting different opinions, and being willing to help when possible. I should also be open to feedback and focus on improving the work rather than taking feedback personally.

If I receive feedback that I do not fully understand, I should ask for clarification. If the same feedback comes up again, I should make sure I understand what needs to change and take action on it.

## Things I Will Avoid

During my internship, I will avoid:

- Waiting around for instructions when I can make reasonable progress myself.
- Coming to meetings without knowing my current progress.
- Multitasking during meetings.
- Asking questions that I could easily answer through basic research.
- Forgetting to take notes or follow up on action items.
- Going silent when I am blocked or unable to complete a task.
- Ignoring feedback.
- Repeatedly asking the same questions because I did not take notes.
- Using an inappropriate communication channel for urgent issues.
- Sending unclear meeting invitations without a useful subject or agenda.

## Reflection

The main thing I learned is that being professional in a remote internship is largely about communication, reliability, and taking responsibility for my own work. I do not need to be overly formal, but I should make sure that my teammates and supervisors know what I am working on, what I have completed, and where I need help.

Going forward, I will try to give regular progress updates, prepare properly for meetings, take better notes, and do some research before asking questions. I will also make sure to follow up on tasks and communicate early when something is blocking my progress.

I have also taken note of the appropriate escalation contact from the onboarding material in case I encounter a serious professional-conduct issue or another situation that requires escalation.
