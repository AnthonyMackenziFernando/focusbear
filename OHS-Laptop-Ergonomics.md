# OHS - Laptop Ergonomics

## Risks of Laptop Use

I learned that using a laptop for long periods can cause problems if the setup is not comfortable. Since the screen is usually below eye level, I tend to look down while working, which can cause neck and shoulder strain. Using the built-in keyboard for a long time can also make the wrists and hands uncomfortable.

Sitting in the same position for several hours can also cause general body discomfort. Looking at the screen continuously can contribute to eye strain and tiredness.

## Ergonomic Equipment

Some equipment that can make a laptop workspace more comfortable includes:

- A laptop stand to raise the screen closer to eye level
- An external keyboard and mouse for better hand positioning
- An external monitor for a more comfortable screen height
- An adjustable chair that provides proper back support
- A footrest if the feet do not rest comfortably on the floor

## Workspace Changes

I raised my laptop using a laptop stand / using laptop charger/books as stand so that the screen was closer to eye level. I also adjusted my chair so that my back was properly supported and my feet were comfortable on the floor.

This made me more aware of how much I was bending my neck while working and encouraged me to keep the screen at a more comfortable height.

## Behavioural Changes

I tried to sit more upright and avoid leaning toward the laptop while working. I also started getting up and moving around after sitting for a long period instead of staying at the desk continuously.

I also made an effort to look away from the screen regularly to give my eyes a short break.

## Focus Bear Reminders

I used Focus Bear reminders to remind me to take movement breaks during the workday. Without a reminder, it is easy to get focused on a task and forget to move for several hours.

The reminders helped me stop for a short break, stand up, move around, and check my posture before getting back to work.

## Reflection

The main change I made was raising my laptop screen and paying more attention to my posture. I noticed that I was previously looking down at the screen quite often, so raising it made the setup more comfortable.

The movement reminders were also useful because they broke up long periods of sitting. I plan to continue using these reminders and keep my laptop setup at a more comfortable height when working for long periods.
